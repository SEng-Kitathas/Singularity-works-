# Design

Preserved product, interaction, visual and QOL contracts. This domain protects earned ideas from archaeology overwrite and keeps rendering aesthetics, project navigation, secure storage, connector authority, and semantic truth in distinct ownership planes.

Current working design contracts:
- `SINGULARITY_WORKS_PRODUCT_TOPOLOGY_v0_1.md` — Singularity Works as product whole; Forge as semantic core; ownership boundaries for Ergo, GitHome, Vault and Connection Gate.
- `SINGULARITY_TRUST_VAULT_CONNECTION_GATE_v0_1.md` — capability-agnostic authority, secure Vault, explicit egress/import quarantine, hybrid-classical/PQ target posture, and connector audit model.
- `GITHOME_PRODUCT_SURFACE_v0_1.md` — GitHub-familiar but project-broader-than-Git workspace/navigation surface with Forge/LBE evidence overlays.

These documents are working App/product architecture contracts. They do not promote App implementation into canonical Forge/Core semantics and do not claim runtime/security enforcement until separately embodied and qualified.
